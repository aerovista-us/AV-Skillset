# AeroVista Brand Boundaries

Shared infrastructure does not imply shared public identity.

## Rules

1. Treat each creator booth, product lane, publication, and division as a standalone public brand unless its configuration explicitly says otherwise.
2. AeroVista or Art Localized may appear as platform/vendor attribution without replacing the primary brand voice.
3. Preserve the configured CTA destination. Never default unrelated CTA buttons to Art Localized, the market, or AeroVista home.
4. Replace generic template language with brand-specific claims only when supported by supplied content.
5. Do not invent member rosters, collaborations, lore, services, pricing, testimonials, availability, or business status.
6. Check title, description, OG metadata, links, QR destinations, navigation, and mobile presentation.
7. For JSON-driven booths, validate schema and confirm the renderer consumes every changed field.

## Review output

Report:

- primary public identity;
- platform attribution;
- CTA destinations checked;
- generic/template language removed;
- claims requiring owner confirmation;
- routes visually verified.
